Privacy Policy — Productivity Map

Last updated: October 1, 2026

What the app does

Productivity Map is a personal notes and goals app. It runs entirely in your browser.

What data we collect

We do not operate any servers and do not collect, receive, sell or share your data. Your notes are stored in your browser's local storage on your device.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app requests one permission: drive.appdata. It lets the app read and write a single file (goalmap.json) in a hidden application folder in your own Google Drive, so your notes sync between your devices. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files. The data goes directly between your browser and Google; it never passes through any server of ours.

Your control

You can sign out in the app at any time, and revoke access in your Google Account (Security → Third-party access). Deleting the app's data from your Google account removes the synced file.
